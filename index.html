What you get

Built for privacy.
Built for people.

Every feature is designed with one rule: your conversations belong to you, not us.

True peer-to-peer encryption

WebRTC creates a direct encrypted tunnel between devices. Our server only helps establish the connection — then it steps out. No call recording. No message logging. No exceptions.

Secure tunnel established P2P
DTLS-SRTP active
Zero server logs

Audio & Video Calls

Crystal-clear audio and HD video. No account, no phone number — just enter a PIN and call.

Private Messaging

Texts, images, voice notes, and files. Messages queue for offline delivery and sync when both sides reconnect.

Group Rooms

Create a shareable link and anyone can join — no account needed. Like Zoom, but with no server in the middle.

Push Notifications

Get notified of incoming calls and messages even when the app is closed. Install to home screen for the full experience.

How it works

Up and running in 3 steps

01
Get your PIN

Open the app and tap "Get your PIN." You're assigned a unique 6-digit number — no email, no password, no phone number.

02
Share it

Give your PIN to whoever you want to reach you. That's your entire identity on iNet — nothing else is needed.

03
Dial & connect

Enter their PIN, choose audio or video, and connect. The call is direct, encrypted, and peer-to-peer from the first ring.

What you get

Built for privacy. Built for people.

Every feature is designed with one rule: your conversations belong to you, not us.

True peer-to-peer encryption

WebRTC creates a direct encrypted tunnel between devices. Our server only helps establish the connection — then it steps out. No call recording. No message logging. No exceptions.

Secure tunnel established P2P
DTLS-SRTP active
Zero server logs

Audio & Video Calls

Crystal-clear audio and HD video calls. No account, no phone number — just a 6-digit PIN shared between you.

In-call Chat

Send messages during any call over the same encrypted data channel. No separate app needed.

File Transfer

Share files directly peer-to-peer. Images, documents, anything — transferred encrypted with no cloud storage.

Group Calls

Create a group room and share a link. Everyone connects peer-to-peer in a mesh. No room server involved.

How it works

Up and running in 3 steps

01
Get your PIN

Open the app and tap "Get your PIN." You're assigned a unique 6-digit number — no email, no password.

02
Share it

Give your PIN to whoever you want to reach you. That's your identity on iNet.

03
Dial & connect

Tap the dial button, enter their PIN, choose audio or video. The call is direct, encrypted, peer-to-peer.
